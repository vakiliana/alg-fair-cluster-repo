\section{Algorithmic Techniques}\label{sec:tech}
\aibanner

A compact toolbox underlies most of the guarantees in this survey. We summarize the recurring techniques and where they appear.

\paragraph{Fairlet decomposition.} Introduced by \citet{DBLP:conf/nips/Chierichetti0LV17}, a \emph{fairlet} is a minimal balanced group of points. The two-phase recipe---partition the data into fairlets, then cluster the fairlet representatives with any off-the-shelf algorithm---decouples fairness from optimization and transfers existing approximation guarantees to the fair setting. The bottleneck is the decomposition itself, which \citet{backurs2019scalable} reduced to a near-linear-time computation via metric embeddings.

\paragraph{LP/convex relaxation and rounding.} The most versatile approach writes an assignment linear program with fairness constraints and rounds it. \citet{bera2019fair} round a standard LP into clusterings with small additive fairness violation for every $\ell_p$ objective; \citet{DBLP:conf/fat/DaiMV22} use tree-metric LPs to obtain purely multiplicative guarantees; and \citet{makarychev2021approximation,DBLP:conf/soda/ChlamtacMV22} design strengthened convex relaxations for the min-max and $(p,q)$-fair objectives. Individually fair clustering is likewise driven by LP rounding~\citep{ChakrabartyN21,VakilianY22}.

\paragraph{Coresets and subsampling.} To scale to massive or streaming data, one computes a \emph{coreset}---a small weighted subset that approximately preserves the (fair) clustering cost. Fairness-aware coresets~\citep{DBLP:conf/waoa/0001SS19,DBLP:conf/nips/HuangJV19,DBLP:conf/icalp/BandyapadhyayFS21} yield constant-factor algorithms that, in some regimes, avoid fairness violations entirely and feed directly into insertion-only and sliding-window streaming pipelines~\citep{cohenfair25}.

\paragraph{Metric tree embeddings.} Probabilistically approximating an arbitrary metric by a distribution over dominating tree metrics~\citep{bartal1998approximating,fakcharoenphol2003tight} turns hard assignment problems into tractable ones on trees, underlying both the near-linear two-group algorithm of \citet{backurs2019scalable} and the multiplicative multi-group bounds of \citet{DBLP:conf/fat/DaiMV22}.

\paragraph{Local search and matroid methods.} Local search---iteratively swapping centers---yields clean bicriteria guarantees for individual fairness~\citep{MahabadiV20} and scales to hundreds of thousands of points via anchor-zone bookkeeping~\citep{bateni2024scalable}; recent anchor-based frameworks~\citep{anchorfair2025} and other scalable pipelines~\citep{fastfairscale2026} push group-fair clustering to very large inputs, and local search also handles individually fair clustering with \emph{outliers}~\citep{lsindivoutliers2025}. When fairness is expressed as a constraint on the \emph{set of centers}, the problem becomes clustering under a matroid constraint, for which LP-with-matroid-polytope rounding, swap/pipage rounding, and matroid-aware local search provide constant-factor approximations~\citep{krishnaswamy2011matroid,chen2016matroid,krishnaswamy2018constant}.

\paragraph{Fixed-parameter (FPT) approximation.} Treating the number of clusters $k$ (and sometimes the number of groups) as a parameter unlocks approximation ratios unattainable in polynomial time. This lens has produced tight FPT guarantees for socially fair clustering~\citep{fptsociallyfair2021,DBLP:journals/ipl/GoyalJ23}, FPT approximations for capacitated and fair clustering with outliers~\citep{fptcapfairoutliers2023}, and parameterized schemes for general norm objectives~\citep{genericnormfpt2023} and for fair-range clustering~\citep{fairrangefpt2026}.

\subsection{Extended objectives and constraints}
Beyond the canonical $k$-center/median/means objectives, several works extend fair clustering to richer settings. \emph{Sum-of-radii} and \emph{min-sum-radii} objectives---which sum cluster radii rather than assignment costs---now admit constant-factor and FPT approximations in the fair setting, both in general metrics~\citep{fairsumradii2025,fptminsumradii2024} and in Euclidean space~\citep{fairminsumradiieuclid2023}, with parameterized results for the (mergeable) sum-of-radii variant~\citep{mergeablesumradii2026}. On the constraint side, recent work handles \emph{doubly constrained} fair clustering that bounds representation from both sides~\citep{doublyconstrained2026}, \emph{minimum-representation} constraints~\citep{minrepconstraints2024}, generalizations to \emph{multiple} protected groups~\citep{multigroupfair2025}, Euclidean $k$-center fairness~\citep{euclideankcenterfair2025}, generalized \emph{colorful} $k$-center~\citep{colorfulkcenter2022}, \emph{diversity-aware} clustering~\citep{diversityaware2024}, fair representation via clustering and consensus~\citep{fairrepconsensus2025}, density-aware individual fairness for $k$-means~\citep{densityindivkmeans2025}, and a fairness notion for the \emph{cluster-editing} problem on graphs~\citep{modfairclusterediting2021}.
